# Story rules (slop is as much about content as looks)

1. **Title = the takeaway, as a sentence.** "Recall rose 12 points with filings added", not "Results". The lint warns on titles under 4 words. No trailing period.
2. **One message per slide.** If a slide needs "and also", split it.
3. **Open with the claim, not the agenda.** The audience should know your point by slide 2 or 3.
4. **Evidence, then meaning.** A number alone is noise; pair each stat with what it means for the audience.
5. **Cut words before shrinking text.** Under about 40 words per slide; the lint warns at 60 and errors at 110. Body stays >= 14pt.
6. **Prefer the visual over the bullet.** Big number, native chart, a flow of steps, a before/after.
7. **No invented facts.** Every number, name, date, quote and source comes from the user's material (the ledger). Missing: write `[TBD: what is needed]` so it is visible. A placeholder deck is fine; a fabricated one is not.
8. **Honest limits slide** for research and technical decks. It builds trust and pre-empts the hard question.
9. **End on a decision or next step**, not "Thank you" or "Questions?".
10. **Speaker notes carry the explanation**, not the slide. Put what you would say there.
11. **Vary rhythm.** Alternate dense and airy, dark and light, type-led and chart-led.
